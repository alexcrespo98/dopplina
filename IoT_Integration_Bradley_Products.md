### Technical Specifications

#### Flow Sensors

The Gems FT110 series flow sensor provides excellent performance for water monitoring applications.

Product documentation available at: [Gems FT110 Series](https://www.gemssensors.com/docs/default-source/resource-files/catalog-pages/catalog-f_ft110-series.pdf?Status=Master&sfvrsn=178e9bc2_10)

*Performance Specifications:*
- Operating pressure: ~2 PSI @ 2.5 LPM
- Temperature range: 0-85°C
- Accuracy: ±3%
